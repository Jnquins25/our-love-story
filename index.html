September 28, 2026 · Our first anniversary

Happy Anniversary, myloveee. ❤️

Sometimes I still can't believe how someone who is so far away from me can feel so close to my heart. We've never even gotten to experience all the normal things couples get to experience together, but somehow, you've still made me feel so loved, understood, and safe.

You're honestly one of the most understanding people I've ever met. You always try to understand me, even when I don't really know how to explain what I'm feeling. You listen to me, you have so much patience with me, and you never make me feel like I'm too much.

You're such a green flag, mylove. 🥹 I really admire how kind you are, how you handle things, and how you understand people. I love how you can understand things without me having to explain every little detail.

And what I love most is that I can feel how much you love me even when you don't say it. I feel it in the way you treat me, the way you talk to me, the way you reassure me, and simply in the way you're always there.

You make me feel safe.

You feel like home to me.

♡

Of course, there are days when I really wish you were here. I want to see you already. I want to hug you, kiss you, cuddle with you, hold your hand, go somewhere together, laugh beside you, and just experience what it's like to have you physically with me.

Sometimes I wish we could skip the distance and just have our little moments together already.

But mylove, I don't want you to feel like you have to rush anything for me.

I don't want you to travel all those miles just because I miss you. I don't want you to feel pressured to make our meeting happen before you're ready. As much as I want to see you, I also want you to focus on yourself, your dreams, your future, and everything you're working hard for. And I'll do mine.

I want you to build the life you're dreaming about.

And I'll be here cheering you on while you do it.

Because when we finally meet, I don't want it to happen because we felt pressured by the distance. I want it to happen when the timing is right, when we're both ready, and when we can actually enjoy that moment without worrying about everything else.

I want our first meeting to be something we can look back on and say, "It was worth waiting for."

So yes, I miss you.

I really, really do.

But I'm also content with what we have right now.

I'm happy with our conversations, our little moments, our late nights, our random talks, our jokes, and all the ways we make each other feel loved even from far away.

I don't need you to prove your love by traveling miles for me.

I already feel your love.

And that's enough for me while we wait.

I know we still have a long way to go. There are things about our future that aren't easy, especially because we're not from the same religion. There are things I haven't even been able to tell my parents yet, and sometimes I don't know exactly how everything will work out.

I'll be honest, that part can be scary.

But even with all of that, when I think about my future, I still hope you're in it.

I want a future with you.

I want to keep supporting you and watching you grow into the person you're meant to become. I want to be there for your successes, your struggles, your dreams, and even the ordinary days.

I want us to keep growing together instead of rushing to get somewhere.

And someday, when the time is finally right, I hope we get to have all those moments we've been imagining.

The first hug.

The first kiss.

The first time we actually get to hold each other's hands.

The cuddles.

The dates.

The random walks.

The stupid little jokes we'd probably laugh about for no reason.

Even just sitting beside each other and doing absolutely nothing.

I want all of that with you.

But until then, I'll be okay loving you from here.

I'll keep supporting you.

I'll keep believing in you.

And I'll keep waiting for the day when the distance between us finally becomes something we can look back on and laugh about.

✦

It's funny how our story started.

I never would have imagined that the person I was dancing beside would become my baby, the person I feel safest with, and the person I would eventually want a future with.

I'm really glad I followed her.

Most of all, I'm really glad I found you.

Our story isn't finished yet.

We still have so many things to experience, so many memories to make, and so many chapters ahead of us.

So I'm not in a hurry.

I'll wait for our time.

And when that time comes, I hope I get to look at you, finally hold you, and think,

"Finally. You're really here."

Until then, mylove, please keep chasing your dreams.

Keep building your future.

Keep becoming the person you're meant to be.

I'll be here, supporting you and loving you along the way. While also chasing my dreams.

Happy Anniversary, my baby. ❤️
